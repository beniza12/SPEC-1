HARRY POTTER

The Boy Who Lived

An adolescent wizard with green eyes, round horn-rimmed glasses, and unruly black hair covering a lightning-shaped scar—the remnant of a wound he received as a baby at the hand of Lord Voldemort.When Harry is eleven, headmaster Albus Dumbledore sends Rubeus Hagrid to retrieve him from the Dursleys, his Muggle (nonmagical) relatives, and bring him to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Harry's closest allies, Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, each bring unique strengths and complexities to their friendship, showcasing themes of loyalty, bravery, and personal growth throughout their adventures.

  • Facts: Belongs to Gryffindor House, loves Quidditch and Hogwarts, owns the Firebolt broomstick, and has a stag Patronus.

  • Skills: Exceptional at Defense Against the Dark Arts, talented Quidditch Seeker, strong leader, and skilled at casting powerful spells like the Patronus Charm.

  • Personality: Brave, loyal, determined, compassionate, and always willing to stand up for what is right.
